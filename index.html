Our Expertise

Star Facade Lighting is a premier provider of specialized LED facade lighting solutions, dedicated to enhancing the aesthetic appeal and visibility of architectural structures across the Middle East, including the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

As an exclusive offering from Trinity Star Eco Energy Solutions, we combine innovative design with energy-efficient LED technology. From the vibrant skylines of Dubai to the expanding architectural landscape of Riyadh, we deliver customized facade lighting strategies that transform buildings into iconic visual experiences.

What We Do

Design

Comprehensive lighting design including 3D visualization, photometric analysis, and technical mapping to bring concepts to reality.

Supply

Direct supply of high-grade, IP-rated LED fixtures and control systems, ensuring quality and longevity for every project.

Installation

Professional on-site installation by certified teams, focused on safety, precision, and seamless integration with the architecture.

AMC

Annual Maintenance Contracts to guarantee system performance, including regular checks, cleaning, and software updates.
